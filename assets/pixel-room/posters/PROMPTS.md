# 像素海报重绘

生成方式：内置 imagegen；每张原图分别作为编辑目标，未使用 CLI/API 回退。原图保留为同名 PNG，重绘原稿为 `*-pixel-source.png`。运行 `node scripts/sprites/prepare-posters.mjs` 生成 48×48、16 色、无抖色的 `*-pixel.png`。Blender 最近邻采样，不裁剪图案。

## omnipotent-youth

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: square wall-poster texture for an existing low-resolution pixel-art bedroom game. Input image is the edit target. Primary request: redraw the attached poster as deliberate coarse hand-drawn pixel art. Preserve its recognizable motif, colors and composition. Style: strict visible square pixels as if drawn on a 48x48 logical grid then enlarged with nearest-neighbor scaling. Sharp flat limited color blocks, no anti-aliasing, no smooth curves, no realism. Full-bleed flat artwork; no wall, no room, no physical paper, no frame, no border, no shadow, no perspective, no watermarks. Keep the ochre mustard field and the bent-over dark human figure in the same dramatic diagonal pose, with the vertical Chinese title “万能青年旅店” on the right. Redraw the figure as chunky charcoal pixel clusters with only three shading tones, no anatomy realism. Preserve the broad silhouette and poster composition. Small secondary type may become simple pixel marks.
